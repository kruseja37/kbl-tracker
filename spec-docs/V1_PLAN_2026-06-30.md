features** are built.

## 5b. IN-SEASON CAP / DEAD-CAP (JK design question 2026-06-30 — ANALYSIS DONE)
Full analysis: **`spec-docs/IN_SEASON_CAP_DEADCAP_ANALYSIS_2026-06-30.md`** (the seed for the missing
`ROSTER_MOVEMENT_GAME_THEORY_SPEC`). Headline: a real dead-cap mechanic is **PARTIAL-BUILT** (send-down
carries 75% of salary as dead money, call-up at full / first-prospect 50%, persisted on every move) but
**TOOTHLESS** — the payroll aggregator that would surface/enforce it has zero consumers, so the penalty is
invisible today. Fan-morale tooth exists (the flashpoint/albatross tax) but is flag-off + accumulate-only.
The two dangerous holes: **free release** (clears the books for nothing → dominates send-down) and
**demotion-as-cap-relief** (75% + a cheap rookie can LOWER your cap — inverts intent). It's mostly a
surface-and-couple job + 3 rulings (call-up scale, release treatment, morale threshold) → folds into the
scouting-intelligence interrogation as ONE economy (in-season dead-cap + draft-time archetype true-cost
must share a cost basis).
