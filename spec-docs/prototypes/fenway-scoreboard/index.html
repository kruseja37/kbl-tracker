Soft or edgy? The scoreboard motif is, by nature, a little industrial — metal, recessed slots, rivets, hard frames — so the full board (Green Monster) reads as the edgy end, and is best saved for hero moments (the hub landing, a game final, the standings). The Whisper end — rivets + a painted placard on an otherwise clean card — sits happily on the soft look and is what you'd sprinkle everywhere. My recommendation: Whisper as the everyday accent, Ballpark for matchups & standings, Green Monster for the home/hero.
