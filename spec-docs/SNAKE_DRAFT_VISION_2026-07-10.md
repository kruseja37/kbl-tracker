## THE CORE OBJECT — THE DRAFT BOARD
Every club has a private, live DRAFT BOARD: your top targeted player at each of the 22
roster slots, forming a viable roster construction — legal AND affordable (IVs + your
archetype's tax) at all times.
- Seeded from your hand-set POSITIONAL RANKINGS; auto-backfill fires ONLY on unavailability
  and promotes YOUR next-ranked name (private notice: "DEXTEREZ GONE — MURASKI STEPS UP").
  Hand placements otherwise never move.
- The what-if sandbox: drag players between slots while waiting; money and tax recompute
  live; keep or revert.
- [v5] **THE BOARD IS THE TAX MODEL.** The planned 22 defines your tax-relevant core — the
  top-N players per taxed ratings category (hitters) and the taxed pitching set — mirroring
  EXACTLY the categories the settlement engine charges on (advisory ≡ settlement, the
  standing law; the board's projected tax uses the same normalized math, byte-for-byte).
  The board ledger shows PROJECTED ALL-IN for the planned 22: salary total + projected tax +
  headroom, updating on every pick made anywhere and every board move you make. A tap-down
  ("YOUR TAX CORE") shows which planned players form each taxed category and what moving
  one does. [RP/CP NOTE for contract time: whether CP counts inside the reliever category
  is a CODE-TRUTH question — the board mirrors whatever the cap tables actually bind on
  (JK's preferred presentation, top-3 RP + 1 CP = 4 bullpen, adopted if the table's
  structure agrees; verified with file evidence before any contract).]
- The board is PRIVATE (companion / reveal). The shared lens never shows it.

## [v5] THE RATIONAL ROOM — the prediction engine
The Asst GM predicts WHEN players will be drafted from the COLLECTIVE ARCHETYPES of the
league — modeling every rival club's rational interests (their archetype's fit-adjusted
worth × their roster needs × their money), not simulated coin flips.
- **Mechanism:** a deterministic rational playout — between now and your next pick, assume
  each club takes its highest rational-interest available player (with legality/money
  rails). Cheap, explainable, reproducible; validated at 8 clubs (v1's launch size; 20 must
  not break, 8 is the tuning target).
- **On your board:** every slot's top target carries a RISK read for YOUR NEXT PICK, coarse
  and reasoned: SAFE ("no rival's rational pick before yours") / AT RISK ("the Comets'
  archetype values him — 1 rational buyer before your turn") / LIKELY GONE ("3 rational
  buyers before your turn"). Counts and reasons, never percentages. First-use copy:
  "Assumes every club drafts to its archetype. Humans surprise."
- **THE ADVISOR LOG [v5]:** a private, append-only diary that writes itself as the draft
  unfolds: risk flips ("DEXTEREZ → LIKELY GONE — 3 rational buyers"), plan hits ("your SS
  slot fell to depth"), and TRADE RECOMMENDATIONS with the guide price attached: "TRADE UP
  TO PICK 9 (GUIDE: YOUR PICKS 14+41) IF YOU DON'T WANT TO MISS DEXTEREZ AS YOUR STARTING
  SHORTSTOP." LLM-dressed one-liners via the proven gated pattern (facts adapter; the LLM
  never invents a number, name, or verdict; template fallback). New-entry badge on your
  private surface; the shared screen never sees it.
- **Convergence note:** this rational-interest model IS the future CPU picker's brain —
  v1.1 CPU clubs simply play the rational room for real. One model, two uses, no drift.

## POSITIONAL RANKINGS — the prep model (carried)
Rank candidates BY POSITION across the 22 spots (auction setup's proven pattern). Advisor
seeds from fit-adjusted worth (one archetype chip + one fit word + TRUE COST per card); you
drag; your touches are frozen forever. Every ranking drives behavior: board slots,
backfills, risk reads, alerts, scarcity math. ELITE/PASS cut (drove nothing); DEPTH = your
lower ranks; VALUE = advice from cost-vs-worth math, not a bucket.

## THE ADVISOR — urgency from arithmetic (carried, sharpened by v5)
Scarcity math + the rational room, spoken one sentence at a time, private only:
"DRAFT A SS SOON — 2 STARTERS LEFT YOU'VE RANKED; 3 CLUBS STILL NEED ONE."
"CF IS SAFE TO WAIT — THE OTHER CLUBS ARE FULL THERE."
"IF YOU PASS ON SS AT THIS PICK, YOUR BOARD SETTLES FOR A DEPTH STARTER."
The lens alert (your own club view, private surface): the LOG's top item, LLM-voiced.

## EVERY CARD SHOWS THE FALLOUT (carried)

YOUR BOARD — SS SLOT" / "OFF-BOARD: bumps your CF plan to depth") · [v5] risk read.

## TRADES — THE GUIDE (carried)
Posted prices per pick slot (chart from the pool's IV curve), always accessible. Trade up:
pick the slot → guide auto-selects which of your picks pay → room nods IRL → COMMISSIONER
pushes through or declines → picks swap, boards recompute, clock moves if the live pick
moved. Legality gate (neither club strands). No custom trades v1; no drafted-player trades
v1 (season TradeFlow owns player trades; v2 candidate). [v5] The LOG's trade recommendations
reference guide prices exactly — the advisor never invents prices.

## SETUP (carried)

shuffle; tap-two-swap; snake preview + endpoint back-to-backs) · GO. No dials.

## [v5.1 ADDENDUM — JK, from the legends thread] PLAYER VERSIONS & ONE-PER-HUMAN
The legends library ships multiple versions of the same human (distinct playerIDs — e.g.,
Ruth: Red Sox two-way / Yankees peak / career). Integration:
1. **Pool-build chooses versions** (the ruled seam): the POOL trim list groups by human
   ("BABE RUTH — 3 CARDS", version picker). Default: all selected versions enter under
   ONE-PER-HUMAN exclusivity.
2. **Draft-time exclusivity:** recording any version retires the human's other versions
   from the pool — one neutral ticker line ("RUTH'S OTHER CARDS LEAVE THE POOL"); every
   board that ranked any version gets the private backfill flow.
3. **HARD INVARIANT — count humans, not cards:** the seating proof, position-supply
   validity, scarcity math, and the rational room must DEDUPE versions under exclusivity
   (a human contributes at most one unit of supply). Dedicated tests; a false-passing
   seating proof here is a stop-ship defect class.
4. **Version legibility:** every card wears its version tag ("BABE RUTH · '27 YANKEES");
   ANY same-name players (natural duplicates included) get a disambiguating chip. Protects
   the wrong-Morales correction case at the source.
5. **Correction:** undoing the pick that retired versions restores them (extends the
   correction-snapshot rules in the states appendix).
6. Rankings/board may hold multiple versions of one human simultaneously (they're
   alternatives); the board's 22-plan counts the human once.

## [v5-FINAL REDLINE — round-4 adoptions, all compatible with JK's rulings]
R1. **Rational-room contract (hard invariant):** inputs = rivals' LOCKED public archetypes
(locked at GO — no mid-draft archetype edits), recorded picks, public roster needs, pick
ownership/order, IV costs, tax rules. NEVER rival rankings, boards, tags, scout bands, or
any private data. Rational interest = fit-adjusted worth × roster need − marginal
true-cost drag (money is a rail and a penalty, never a desire multiplier); a rival's
projected pick must leave them a legal, affordable finish. Drafting against your archetype
to fool the room is legitimate counterplay, by design. Copy speaks PRESSURE, never intent:
the badge is "NEXT PICK — SAFE TO WAIT" / "AT RISK" / "LIKELY GONE" (the word SAFE never
stands alone), reasons behind a tap ("2 rational buyers before your turn").
R2. **The LOG on an actionability leash:** appends ONLY material events — a target crossing
LIKELY GONE, a slot falling to depth (with the action attached: "…draft Muraski now or
pursue the guide trade for pick 9"), a newly EXECUTABLE guide trade, the board going
illegal/unaffordable. Max one material change per target between your turns. One live
sentence displayed (the lens alert IS the LOG's top item — no separate alerts feed).
Canonical template text stored; the LLM dresses only the displayed sentence. Badge only
unseen actionable entries. Expired recommendations keep their history line but lose their
action ("EXPIRED — pick 9 no longer available").
R3. **Trade recommendations come from the validator, not the chart:** an offered package
must be executable NOW — picks owned and unspent, balancing return picks included so both
clubs keep legal roster counts ("OFFER 14+41; RECEIVE 9+62 — guide-matched and legal now"),
both clubs retain a legal finish, revalidated at commissioner approval. If no legal package
exists: "No legal guide trade reaches pick 9." Language: "the active turn changes if
ownership of the live pick changes" (there is no clock).
R4. **Two bills, two names, never confused:** the BOARD shows PLAN COST · PLAN TAX · PLAN
CUSHION ("if these 22 remain available"); the CANDIDATE shows LEGAL-FINISH CUSHION ("after
this pick and the cheapest legal finish"). The word "headroom" appears on neither. Help
copy: "Plan Cushion prices your chosen 22. Legal-Finish Cushion protects the cheapest
roster you can still complete."
R5. **Board integrity invariants:** the board = 22 UNIQUE playerIDs (a multi-position
player occupies one slot); slot reassignment of the same 22 can change LEGALITY but never
TAX (only membership changes the bill — spec bug fixed); backfill promotes the next UNUSED
legal target or declares PLAN BROKEN — it never moves another frozen placement.
R6. **The sandbox is WHAT-IF, never a solver:** shows only the user's chosen change vs the
saved board (Plan cost −$18k · Plan tax +$31k · Plan cushion −$13k → KEEP / REVERT). No
OPTIMIZE button, no auto-best replacements, no exhaustive comparisons, no rational-room-
ranked substitutes.
R7. **Farm speaks SCOUT PRESSURE:** each GM's playout uses ONLY their own scouting snapshot
+ public facts; different scouts read differently; copy = "YOUR SCOUT: HIGH PRESSURE —
Comets pick twice before you and publicly need a shortstop." CODE-TRUTH CHECK at contract
time: if per-club scout variance doesn't exist in the engine, farm shows public role
pressure only (no named-player reads) until it does.
R8. **Board default view, stripped (pixel priority):** (1) current pick, your next pick,
one advisor sentence · (2) 22 slots (position, target, owned/available/gone) · (3) danger
badges only (AT RISK / LIKELY GONE — no 22 green badges) · (4) PLAN COST · TAX · CUSHION ·
(5) the WHAT-IF keep/revert bar while active. Behind taps: full order, positional rankings
(opened FROM a slot), buyer counts/reasons, YOUR TAX CORE, the LOG (separate sheet, one
new-action badge, top sentence echoed above the board). Backfill = one toast + one LOG
receipt, no notice panel.
